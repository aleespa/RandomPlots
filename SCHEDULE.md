# Reel schedule

Posting days: **every day except Wednesday** (Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun), through
2026-12-31 (69 Reels). The first four (2026-10-12 to 10-16) went out on the earlier
Mon, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sun plan; the six-day week starts on 2026-10-17.
Each Reel lives in `lab/2026/<MMDD>.py`, named after its posting date.
2026-10-20 is the account's 400th post, a special Reel outside the category rotation; every
Reel from there on moved one slot later, keeping its category (the rotation continues unbroken).

Status values: `todo` → `pitched` → `in progress` (picture being iterated) → `video ok`
(picture approved) → `music ok` (soundtrack chosen) → `posted`. Fill in the title once the
pitch is picked. If a Reel is made on a different day than it posts, keep the script named
after the posting date.

## Categories

Each slot has a category, and its pitches stay inside that category. The categories rotate
in the fixed order below, so each one comes back every 15 posts (two and a half weeks) and no two
neighbouring posts share one. To swap a slot's category, trade it with another slot so the
counts stay balanced.

| # | Category | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Waves & Quantum | Schrödinger evolution, interference, standing waves, wave packets |
| 2 | Fractals | Julia/Mandelbrot zooms, IFS, Newton fractals, L-systems |
| 3 | Number Theory | primes, modular multiplication circles, Ulam spirals, continued fractions |
| 4 | Fluids & Flow Fields | Navier–Stokes, vortices, advected dye, flow-field particles |
| 5 | Tilings & Geometry | Penrose/aperiodic tilings, Voronoi, packings, polyhedra |
| 6 | Chaos & Attractors | Lorenz/Rössler/Clifford attractors, double pendulums, bifurcations |
| 7 | Graphs & Networks | spanning trees, mazes, shortest paths, force-directed graphs |
| 8 | Complex Analysis | domain colouring, conformal maps, Riemann surfaces, Möbius maps |
| 9 | Cellular Automata | Life, Lenia, elementary rules, sandpiles |
| 10 | Curves & Differential Geometry | spirographs, curvature flow, geodesics, evolutes, envelopes |
| 11 | Probability & Randomness | random walks, Brownian motion, DLA, Galton boards, CLT |
| 12 | Topology & Higher Dimensions | knots, Möbius strips, Hopf fibration, 4D rotations and slices |
| 13 | Pattern Formation | reaction–diffusion (Gray–Scott, Turing), phyllotaxis, flocking |
| 14 | Optics & Billiards | caustics, ray tracing, mathematical billiards, lenses |
| 15 | Fourier & Harmonics | epicycles, Lissajous, harmonographs, Chladni plates |
